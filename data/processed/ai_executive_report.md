*   **Inefficient Customer Acquisition Spend:** With 33.92% of customers classified as 'Lost', there is a risk that resources invested in customer acquisition are not yielding sustainable long-term value, leading to a high Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) relative to Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV).
*   **Stagnant Customer Lifetime Value (CLTV) Growth:** The minimal presence of 'Loyal', 'Champion', and 'VIP Wholesale' customers suggests difficulty in nurturing customers through the loyalty lifecycle, limiting overall CLTV and long-term business growth.
*   **Competitive Vulnerability:** A large segment of 'Regular' customers combined with high average recency and substantial churn risk makes the customer base highly susceptible to competitors actively targeting disengaged or semi-active customers.
*   **Negative Brand Perception:** A consistently high churn rate can lead to negative word-of-mouth and online reviews, which can damage brand reputation and make future customer acquisition efforts more challenging and costly.
